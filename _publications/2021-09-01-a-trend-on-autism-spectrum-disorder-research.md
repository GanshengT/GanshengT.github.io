Human electrophysiology captures the neural dynamics, while eye tracking demarcates the oculomotor events such as saccades, fixations, and blinks, that mark the boundaries between cognitive operations. Combining them provides a uniquely powerful window into how the brain sequences and switches between cognitive processes over time.
