This systematic review synthesizes evidence on high-intensity and low-intensity focused ultrasound as a neuromodulation approach for major depressive disorder. Across animal studies and early human work, ultrasound shows promise as a noninvasive intervention while highlighting the need for careful parameter selection and stronger clinical evidence.
