^1: By *Eastern fantasy novels*, I refer specifically to **修真小说 (xiūzhēn xiǎoshuō)**, literally “novels of cultivating truth.” These works depict characters who pursue physical and metaphysical refinement—training mind, body, and spirit to attain harmony with the Dao (道) and, often, immortality. 
In English, they are commonly known as **cultivation novels**. The broader genre includes several subcategories, such as **仙侠 (xiānxiá)**: “immortal hero” fiction, combining martial arts, Taoist philosophy, and transcendence.
